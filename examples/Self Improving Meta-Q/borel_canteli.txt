### 🧠 **Borel–Cantelli Lemma**:

**1st Lemma (non-dependent):**

> If
> [
> \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \mathbb{P}(A_n) < \infty,

> then
> [
> \mathbb{P}(\limsup A_n) = 0
> ]
> i.e., the event happens only **finitely often** with probability 1.

**2nd Lemma (independent events):**

> If the events (A_n) are **independent** and
> [
> \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \mathbb{P}(A_n) = \infty,
> ]
> then
> [
> \mathbb{P}(\limsup A_n) = 1
> ]
> i.e., the event happens **infinitely often** with probability 1.
